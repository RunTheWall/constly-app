# Roadmap

Constly ships one release at a time. This is direction, not a schedule: no
dates, and priorities move with what people actually ask for. The best way to
shape it is to open an issue, or give a thumbs-up to one that already exists.

## Recently shipped

- Printing on Windows and Linux, through your system's print dialog (4.14)
- Proxy support for company networks, and settings IT teams can manage with a
  macOS configuration profile or Windows Group Policy (4.13)
- A quick-insert menu on `/`, pictures you paste or drop in, and GitHub's
  callouts (4.12)
- Word, ODT, RTF, LaTeX and EPUB import and export without installing anything
  first, and Google Docs import (4.10)
- Zoom, page setup for PDF and Word, and Mermaid diagrams in the PDF (4.9)
- Pictures from outside the document's folder or from the web, after one
  question, and Word import with its tables and pictures (4.8)
- Open in Constly from VS Code, and from Cursor, Windsurf and VSCodium through
  Open VSX (4.7)

## What we are working toward

- **Wider reach.** More package managers and app stores, so Constly is one
  command away wherever you already install things.
- **Cleaner exits.** More export formats, so a finished document leaves Constly
  intact.
- **Fewer small frictions.** The daily-writing rough edges, reported in Issues
  and fixed in order.

For what has already landed, see the [release notes](CHANGELOG.md). Have
something you want? [Open an issue](https://github.com/RunTheWall/constly-app/issues/new/choose).
